# The MishMash Mesh Manifesto (MMN)

*Draft 0.1 · 2026-11-27 · mishmashmesh.net · mishmashmesh.network*

## Why

The internet and mobile networks have become the bloodstream of our lives. When they stop, in a flood, a blackout, a war, censorship or a simple outage, we lose contact with our families, with help and with the world in an instant.

We believe **connectivity is a commons**. It should not depend on one operator, one cable or one switch.

## What MishMash Mesh is

MishMash Mesh is an open, grassroots network built by people from what they already own: old WiFi routers, cheap single-board computers, LoRa modules and two-way radios.

Every MMN node broadcasts an `MMN-…` hotspot. Anyone who joins it can write, talk and share information with others on the network, **even when the internet and cell towers are down**.

MMN does not reinvent everything. **We connect what already exists:** WiFi mesh, Meshtastic, MeshCore, Reticulum, amateur radio, CB and FM, SMS, the internet and messaging apps, into one network with one simple installer.

## What we believe

1. **Resilience over convenience.** The network must work when everything else fails. Every function must work locally, without a central server.
2. **Anyone can join.** An old router, a Raspberry Pi and a little time are enough to become part of the network.
3. **Openness.** Code, protocols, documentation and the node map are open. Anyone can inspect, improve and replicate them.
4. **Bridges, not walls.** A message from Meshtastic should reach MeshCore, an SMS, a radio or a messenger. Technologies should talk to each other.
5. **Privacy by design.** We encrypt wherever the law allows. We do not collect data we do not need.
6. **Honesty about physics.** We tell the truth about range and throughput. Fast locally, slower globally, but something always gets through.
7. **Respect for the law and the spectrum.** We follow the rules of the radio bands, including amateur and CB. We do not interfere with others.
8. **Shared value.** Those who build and maintain the network should benefit in real terms: recognition, services and, in the future, compensation for the documented work of their nodes.
9. **Community before token.** Money can help us grow, but it is not the goal. People and a working network come first.

## How it works (in brief)

1. Take an unused, working WiFi router.
2. Connect a small computer running the MMN software to it.
3. The node links to neighbouring nodes (WiFi, LoRa, radio, the internet if available).
4. The router starts broadcasting an `MMN-xxxx` hotspot.
5. Anyone in range can use the independent network: chat, map, local information, gateways to other systems.

## Who we are talking to

- **Radio amateurs**, who have built operator-independent communications for a hundred years.
- The **Meshtastic, MeshCore and Reticulum** communities, who proved that cheap mesh communication works.
- **Community network** builders such as Freifunk and Guifi.net.
- **Municipalities, fire brigades, emergency services and aid organisations** that need communications in a crisis.
- **Anyone** with an old router in a drawer who wants to do something good.

## How you can help

- Put up an MMN node at home, on a roof, at a school or a fire station.
- Test the range and add your node to the map.
- Help with code, documentation, translations, antenna and enclosure design.
- Build a gateway for your technology: Meshtastic, MeshCore, DMR, CB, APRS, SMS.
- Tell your community about MMN.

**Connectivity belongs to people. Let's build it together.**

*MishMash Mesh: every router can be a bridge.*
